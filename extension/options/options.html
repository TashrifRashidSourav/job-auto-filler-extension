Site rules

When you correct a field in the preview, the choice is remembered for that website (and Gemini's guesses are cached too). Delete anything that's wrong.

Rules are just "this field on this website = that profile field", with no personal data, so you can share them with friends who apply on the same portal.

Gemini AI (optional)

The extension works fully without a key. A key adds: smarter matching for unusual fields, and importing free-form documents. Get a free key at Google AI Studio. The key is stored only in this browser and is used only to call Google's Gemini API. Only field labels are sent for matching. Dropdown picking sends the single value being selected (e.g. an upazila name) together with that dropdown's options. When you import a document with AI, its text is sent to Google.

Filling

By default the extension only touches the tab you click it on. Some job portals embed their application form from another website (an iframe); allowing all sites lets the extension fill those too. It still only runs when you click it or press the shortcut.

Keyboard shortcuts: Alt+Shift+F fill, Alt+Shift+P preview. Change them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
